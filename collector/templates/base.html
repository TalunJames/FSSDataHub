How this works

  1. The crawler reads. It visits official county and city sites, archives every page, and hands them to the AI.
  2. A second AI checks. It re-reads each answer against the archived page. Anything it confirms goes straight into the database.
  3. You settle the rest. Only what it could not confirm reaches the Review page.

Nothing here is published outside the database, so it is safe to send a place back to the crawler whenever you are unsure.
